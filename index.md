## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in [Biophysics](https://biophysics.fas.harvard.edu/) at Harvard University, advised by Prof. [Cengiz Pehlevan](https://pehlevan.seas.harvard.edu/), and a Graduate Fellow at the [Kempner Institute](https://kempnerinstitute.harvard.edu/) for the Study of Natural and Artificial Intelligence.

I study the theory of neural computation. My work asks how recurrent neural networks arrive at the solutions they do, what alternative solutions exist beyond the usual simplicity bias, and what these dynamics tell us about computation in biological circuits.

Before Harvard, I studied physics, computer science, and mathematics at the University of Pennsylvania, and spent a year as a machine learning engineer at PathAI.

## Research Interests

- **Theory of neural computation:** dynamics and solution spaces of recurrent neural networks
- **Computational neuroscience:** data-constrained models of neural dynamics and their mechanistic interpretation
- **Machine learning:** inductive biases, learning dynamics, and generalization
